## Adding a provider to an existing engine

In 2026 I was responsible for the Air Europa NDC integration within a multi-provider flight engine. I worked as a Software Engineer for a B2B travel platform.

The engine already existed and was shared with the team. My work had two separate parts: implementing the provider integration, and leading the .NET migration as the sole engineer responsible for four critical engine repositories.

## Seven operations

I implemented AirShopping, OfferPrice, OrderCreate, OrderRetrieve, OrderChange, OrderCancel and UpSell against PROS, over SOAP/XML with manual builders and parsers.

From search to order: the ticket illustrates offer search, price confirmation and order creation on a fictional itinerary.

## Contracts and tracing

The integration required mapping the provider contract to the engine's domain: baggage, fare types, age codes and multi-segment journeys. I also worked on HTTP handling, tracing with Serilog, Grafana and Loki, payload storage in Azure Blob and multi-repository releases in Azure DevOps.

## Resolving error 490

A 490 error response was resolved by fixing the DataLists wrapper. The change was in the structure of the message sent to the service.

## Engine migration

The migration moved the engine from .NET 6 to .NET 10 and covered four critical repositories, described by responsibility: the flight engine, the engine backend, expenses and ticketing. I delivered them validated in production. To verify it, I checked byte-for-byte A/B parity across the evaluated case set and the write cycle, comparing the migrated behaviour with the previous engine.

## Delivery

The internal work record reports successful offer retrieval and all seven operations in production with real agencies. Client code, payloads and traces are private.
