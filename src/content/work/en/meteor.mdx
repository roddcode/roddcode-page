## A CRM for the Movistar account

From December 2024 to December 2025 I worked at VML (WPP) on the Movistar account. METEOR was an in-house CRM for leads, queries and reports, intended to replace reliance on InConcert.

## My contribution

I worked as a Fullstack Engineer and was the only frontend engineer on the team, reporting to the CTO. I built views and components with Vue.js and Tailwind, connected REST APIs and worked with C#/.NET and SQL Server in an MVC architecture.

My work included query filters, report views, landing pages and campaign emails, along with traceable incident handling. The CRM was a team delivery.
