## A CRM for the Movistar account

From December 2024 to December 2025 I worked at VML (WPP) on the Movistar account. METEOR was my SENATI thesis, developed within VML. The account used InConcert only as a frontend and had a monthly cost; much of the backend and report generation already lived in SQL Server. The project was to build an in-house CRM that would consume and extend that base to manage leads, queries and reports.

## My contribution

I worked as a Fullstack Engineer and was the only frontend developer assigned to METEOR, within a larger account team. I reported to the PM responsible for that area on Movistar. I built the CRM on top of that base: views and components with Vue.js and Tailwind, REST API integration, and work with C#/.NET and SQL Server in an MVC architecture.

My work included query filters, report views, landing pages and campaign emails, along with traceable incident handling. The CRM was a team delivery.
